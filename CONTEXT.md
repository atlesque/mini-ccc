# Mini CCC context

## Schedule

- **Agenda** — the locally saved arrangement of schedule blocks inside the 10:00–22:00 event window.
- **Schedule block** — one pre-made activity that occupies one or more half-hour slots. A block is at least 30 minutes and at most four hours, and blocks cannot overlap.
- **Available block** — a pre-made activity that has not yet been placed in the agenda. Each available block can be used once per agenda.
- **Open time** — a gap with no schedule block. Lunch is represented as open time, not as a draggable block.

## Catalogues

- **Build** — a Mini CCC electronics project with a parts list and estimated cost.
- **Watch item** — a curated external YouTube recommendation shown on the Videos page.
- **Cocktail** — a Club-Mate drink recipe. The collection includes both alcoholic and alcohol-free serves.
